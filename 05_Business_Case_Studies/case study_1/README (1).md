# 🛍️ Retail Sales Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Summary
This project analyzes retail sales performance using a dataset of **500 transactions**. The goal is to uncover meaningful business insights across multiple dimensions such as:

- Time
- Region
- Product
- Sales Channels

Data processing and aggregation were performed using **SQL**, while an **interactive dashboard** was built in **Power BI** to visualize the findings effectively.

### 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- 💰 Total Revenue  
- 🧾 Total Transactions  
- 👥 Total Customers  
- 📊 Average Order Value (AOV)  

---

## 📊 Analytical Findings

### 📈 Sales Trends
- Revenue is generally consistent.
- Noticeable spikes during certain months indicate **seasonal buying behavior**.

### 🌍 Regional Performance
- Sales are evenly distributed across regions.
- Indicates a **balanced market presence**.

### 🛒 Product Contribution
- A small number of products generate a large share of revenue.
- Highlights the importance of **top-performing products**.

### 💻 Sales Channels
- Both **online** and **offline** channels perform well.
- Online sales show stronger performance during specific periods.

### 🏷️ Category Insights
- Different categories perform differently.
- Reflects **changing customer preferences and demand trends**.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations

- 📅 Align marketing campaigns with high-demand periods.
- 📢 Strengthen digital marketing to boost online sales.
- 🎯 Implement customer retention strategies (e.g., loyalty programs).
- 🔍 Optimize underperforming categories (pricing, promotions, product mix).
- 📊 Continuously monitor KPIs using dashboards for better decision-making.

---

## 🧾 Final Remarks

The Power BI dashboard provides a **comprehensive view of sales performance**, enabling:

- Quick insights  
- Data-driven decisions  
- Improved business strategies  

Consistent monitoring of these insights will support **long-term growth** and **better customer satisfaction**.
